Neon Palms is an original open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal city of Palmera. Explore on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and run a six-mission story for fixer Sol Vega.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- WASD to drive or walk; E to enter a car and interact.
- Aim with the mouse; click or press F to fight or shoot.
- Shift to sprint, Space for handbrake, P to pause, M to mute.

## What's in the game

- Explore Palmera's avenues, beach, marina, airport, docks, and city blocks.
- Collect hidden packages, find shops and safehouses, and meet the city's factions.
- Build heat through crime and evade police pursuit.
